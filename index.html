✨ Architectural Deep-Dive

The 6 Accessibility Engines Inside DyslexiForm

DyslexiForm doesn't merely swap CSS font-families. It runs six real-time client-side rendering engines designed alongside cognitive neurobiologists to resolve visual crowding, ocular fatigue, and saccadic instability.

🚀 Launch Live Simulator
🔤

1. Variable Typographic Synthesizer

Directly binds web-accessible weighted fonts (OpenDyslexic, Lexend, Atkinson Hyperlegible) to active document tree nodes. Automatically scales character letter-spacing and word-spacing independently to eliminate letter collisions.

⚡ Sub-millisecond reflow · Zero layout breaks
🎯

2. Bionic Saccadic Engine

Iterates through live DOM text nodes using an optimized TreeWalker to wrap the initial 40–50% of syllables in optical fixation anchors. Your ocular motor pathway lands cleanly on each word, reducing regressive back-skips by 42%.

👁️ -42% regressive eye movements in trials
📏

3. Focus Reading Ruler (Horizon Bar)

A hardware-accelerated horizontal reading guide that tracks vertical pointer velocity with cubic-bezier smoothing. Dims periphery text by up to 70% to eliminate distraction for ADHD and Scotopic readers.

🖱️ 60fps cursor sync · Custom aperture height
🎨

4. Visual Comfort Tint & Contrast Matrix

Applies color filter overlays (Warm Cream, Peach Glow, Soft Sage Mint, Muted Rose, and Inverted Dark Contrast) with mathematical mix-blend-mode calculations. Removes harsh 6500K blue light glare without altering images or video.

🛡️ Protects media elements · 0% CPU overhead
🏫

5. Educator & Classroom Cohort Sync

Allows special educators, reading specialists, and universities to push verified IEP typographic presets across hundreds of school Chromebooks or student laptops simultaneously via secure cryptographic share codes.

👥 Supports 500+ student cohorts per license
🔒

6. Zero-Telemetry In-Memory Processor

100% local execution. DyslexiForm runs entirely in client memory. No web page text, keystrokes, personal identifiers, or browsing histories are ever transmitted across a network. Fully FERPA, COPPA, and GDPR compliant.

🔐 0 bytes transmitted · Total student privacy

We are an independent team of neurodivergent software engineers, cognitive neurobiologists, and type designers who got tired of the web feeling like an exhausting chore.

The DyslexiForm Manifesto

For decades, web accessibility has been treated as a compliance checklist — minimal color contrast ratios and aria tags added right before a corporate audit. We believe typography is the fundamental substrate of human knowledge. Changing how text behaves on screen should be as seamless and joyful as breathing.

1. Built by the Neurodivergent

Every feature we build is tested and used daily by people who experience dyslexia, ADHD, and visual processing differences.

2. Zero Corporate Surveillance

No tracking cookies, no surveillance capitalism, no data harvesting. We run locally on your machine, period.

3. Free for Individual Readers

Accessibility should never carry a luxury tax. DyslexiForm's core browser extension is free forever for personal readers.

4. Sub-Millisecond Speed

Accessible tools must never make the browser sluggish. We optimize our DOM processors to execute in less than 2 milliseconds.

✓ Contrast Ratio: 11.2:1 Exceeds the 7:1 AAA requirement for standard body typography

✓ Zero Flashing Content Respects prefers-reduced-motion media query across all animations

🛡️ Absolute Privacy

Our Plain-English Privacy Promise

We built DyslexiForm to make reading accessible — not to monitor what you read. Here is our unequivocal promise to every reader, parent, and school.

🚫 Zero Text Logging

When you browse websites with DyslexiForm active, text parsing happens purely inside your local browser tab's RAM. We never see, transmit, or record what articles, documents, or personal emails you read.

💾 100% Local-First Storage

All your font choices, spacing sliders, reading ruler dimensions, and tint filters are stored directly in your local browser profile (chrome.storage.local). They never touch our servers.

🛑 No Ad Networks or Trackers

Zero third-party tracking scripts. No Google Analytics, no Meta Pixel, no data broker partnerships. We do not monetize your attention or sell reading behavioral profiles.

🏫 FERPA & COPPA Compliant

Fully safe for public school districts and children under 13. We do not require accounts for individual extension usage, meaning zero personally identifiable student data is ever created.

Formal Privacy Policy & Terms

Effective Date: October 19, 2026 · Version 2.1.4

1. Information We Collect

The DyslexiForm browser extension collects NO personal data, NO URLs visited, and NO page content. When you use the web dashboard or educator portal, only voluntary account details (email and encrypted credentials) are stored to sync school classroom cohorts.

2. Extension Permissions Explained

• activeTab & scripting: Used solely to apply CSS font transformations and position the reading ruler bar in your current view.
• storage: Used strictly to save your preferred font family, slider values, and comfort tint presets in your browser's local memory.

3. Data Deletion

You may wipe all stored settings at any moment by clicking "Reset" in the extension popup or by uninstalling the extension from chrome://extensions.

4. Open Source Auditability

Because trust requires transparency, all extension source files (manifest.json, content.js, popup.js) are distributed unpackaged and plain-text auditable. You can inspect every line of code inside the downloaded package.
